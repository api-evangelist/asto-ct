The Melbourne Cup, one of the most prestigious horse racing events in the world, has been at the forefront of this positive change. Over the past three years, the implementation of pre-race screening protocols with weight-bearing CT scans has played a pivotal role in ensuring the safety of horses and riders, resulting in zero equine fatalities during this period.
