# Asto CT

> Asto CT, Inc. is a Middleton, Wisconsin medical-imaging device manufacturer that builds Equina, a robotically positioned, weight-bearing fan-beam computed tomography (CT) scanner purpose-built for imaging mildly sedated, standing horses. Asto CT sells capital imaging equipment plus installation, training and ongoing clinical support to equine hospitals and veterinary teaching hospitals.
